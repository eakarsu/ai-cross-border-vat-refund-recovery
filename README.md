# Cross-Border VAT Refund Recovery

Recover foreign VAT from eligible business expenses through country-specific evidence, deadlines, and refund submissions.

**Primary buyer:** Multinational companies and travel-heavy enterprises. **Evidence:** supplier invoices, VAT registrations, expense categories, business purpose, country rules, deductible percentages, original documents, portals, deadlines, claims, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Entity VAT profile
- Supplier invoice ingestion
- Invoice validity checks
- Country eligibility rules
- Expense category classification
- Business purpose evidence
- Partial deductibility calculation
- Domestic registration exclusion
- Original document control
- Minimum claim threshold
- Filing deadline monitoring
- Portal claim preparation
- Tax authority query workflow
- Refund reconciliation
- Country category analytics
